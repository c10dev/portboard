# Logos

`icon.svg` is the app icon: three rounded white bars (rows of a port list) and a green dot on a blue square with 7px
corners, drawn on a 32 × 32 grid. Its colours are fixed: blue `#2f6feb`, white `#fff` and green `#5fe08d`. They are not
UI tokens and do not change with the theme. The icon reads on light and dark grounds alike. Use it at 16px and up,
as is: do not recolour or crop it. Portboard has no wordmark; set the name in the
`sans` family next to the icon.
