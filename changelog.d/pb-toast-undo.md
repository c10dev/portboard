### Changed
- **A toast stack** ([docs/layout](docs/layout/README.md#toasts-undo-and-row-actions)): toasts now stack bottom right, 360 px wide, with the newest in front and the rest tucked behind. Each stays at least 6 s; the old 2.5–3.5 s ones now last 6 s. A toast with an action stays until it is used or closed. Repeats merge as "(×3)". Screen readers announce them politely. `toast(text, error, { link, ms })` still works, and the options form adds `action` and `key`.
- **Undo instead of a confirm for reversible changes**: unpinning, hiding, clearing or changing a label, and turning a port or device down alert off apply at once with a 되돌리기 toast that re-posts the previous prefs exactly. Stop, restart, worktree removal and tailnet 공개 keep their confirm.
- **Row actions on hover**: in dense 서비스 rows, ⋯ shows on hover, focus or selection and while its menu is open, and always on touch. The "◷ n분 전 사용" meta gives way on hover. A right-click on a row opens the same menu, a press outside closes it, and destructive items (중지) come last, after a separator, in red.
