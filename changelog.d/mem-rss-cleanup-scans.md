### Fixed
- Worktrees: removing or pruning worktrees no longer forces a full scan per batch. The server now queues one incremental scan that starts after the removal. Further batches while it waits share that scan. The dashboard waits for it instead of asking for `?refresh`. Before, the scan the server started after a removal could be one that was already running and had read the repo before the change. The dashboard's forced full scan covered for that, but only once a minute, so a second batch soon after could leave removed worktrees on the page until the next 15-minute scan. A rescan plus three batches (64 of 125 worktrees) now runs one full scan instead of two. This cuts work, not memory. The RSS climb during such a cleanup (167 → 309 MB in a replay: young generation 4 → 128 MB, plus ~20 MB of old space and malloc, all released within minutes) is bounded by the unit's `--max-semi-space-size=16` (200 → 205 MB).
