### Added
- **Resizable sidebar and inspector** ([docs/layout](docs/layout/README.md#resizable-panes-and-one-shortcut-registry)): drag a pane's inner edge (3 px before it counts) to set the sidebar to 200–420 px and the inspector to 280–480 px. Drag the sidebar under 100 px and it collapses; a click on its edge toggles it. The handles are keyboard separators (← →, Home / End, Enter). The widths are remembered in `pb.shell` and applied before the first paint. The list keeps at least 324 px, so wider panes move the breakpoints up: at both widest, the sidebar is a column from 752 px and the inspector from 1232 px. At the default widths the layout is unchanged.
- **One shortcut registry and a ⌘/ 키보드 단축키 dialog**: `public/lib/keys.js` defines every shortcut once (⌘K, /, ⌘\, Esc, ↑/↓, ⌘/, 뒤로/앞으로), and the same entries drive the key handlers, the palette's 명령 rows (now showing their keys) and the dialog. Keys read as ⌘ glyphs on a Mac and in the Mac app, and as Ctrl words elsewhere. In the Mac app the dialog also lists the app's own menu keys (⌘1–4, ⌘[, ⌘R, ⇧⌘O); a test keeps the page's shortcuts off them.
