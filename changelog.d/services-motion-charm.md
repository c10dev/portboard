### Changed
- The 서비스 tab moves with the app's own motion steps: selecting a row, and opening or closing the inspector, use the `dur-1`, `dur-2`, `ease-out` and `ease-in` tokens. A selection made with ↑/↓ stays instant. The README motion table in `design-system/` lists them.
- The selected row's port number is accent-coloured, a pressed row takes the selected background, the empty inspector shows ↑ and ↓ as key chips, and a row flashes once when a port with a down alert comes back up.
