### Added
- **Unused services as suggestions**: `/api/suggestions` and `list_suggestions` gain `kind: "idle"` for a listening service nobody has connected to for 14 days according to the 30-day usage history, whether it runs as an installed systemd unit, a docker container or a plain process. Each one carries its owner, last use and the device it came from; the 서비스 page shows "💤 N일째 안 씀" on the row with 무시 only. Portboard never stops anything for it. Pinned, hidden, system and Portboard's own ports are left out, and nothing is suggested until the history spans 14 days; a port the history never saw needs its service to be up that long (#3).
