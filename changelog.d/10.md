### Added
- `portboard agent` (`src/agent.ts`, `src/agent/server.ts`): a light, read-only agent for every other machine. It serves `GET /agent/v1/info` (version, host, load), `/agent/v1/ports` (listening sockets per bind address, and the remote connector's redacted port list) and `/agent/v1/usage` on :8797, bound to loopback and the Tailscale IP. Readers: loopback, a bearer token (`agent.token`, `PORTBOARD_AGENT_TOKEN`), or a Tailscale identity on `agent.allow` (node name or ID, login, `tag:`), checked with `tailscale whois`; with neither, loopback only. No pids, command lines, paths or LAN addresses leave the machine. `portboard install-service --agent` writes `portboard-agent.service` alone (#10).
- The board reads every agent in portboard.json's `agents` (`name`, `url`, optional `token`; `src/core/agents.ts`): at most every 10 s, each agent backing off on its own (15 s up to 5 min) with fixed error codes and its last good ports kept. Agent ports go on the tailnet device of that name (or a card of their own) ahead of compute-pool's, and show in the 기기 tab and the host picker with the agent's state and load; `/api/devices` carries `agent` per device, `portsSource: "agent"` and `agents[]` (#10).
